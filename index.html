About the company

Your safety. Our priority.

Bassoon Limited is a trusted supplier of high-quality personal protective equipment (PPE) and hygiene solutions tailored for the food industry. We specialise in a comprehensive range of safety products — masks, HDPE gloves, latex gloves, hairnets, dustcoats, safety boots, cold room suits and overalls — all designed to meet hygiene and safety standards. We also supply office supplies and stationery for all your office needs.Committed to excellence, we ensure that our products enhance workplace safety,prevent contamination and comply with food industry regulations. Whether for food processing, packaging or handling. Bassoon Limited delivers reliable, durable and cost effective PPE Solutions to safe guard both your workforce and products.
